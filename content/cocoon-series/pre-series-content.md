## PART A — 3 TEASERS (hint at the series without spoiling)

### Teaser 1 — "The Scared Writer"

**Format:** Single-slide text post on black background OR 15-second talking-to-camera reel.

**Copy:**
> "I'm writing something I've been scared to write for 20 years.
> I don't know if I'll post it.
> I'll decide this weekend."

**Why it works:** Pulls empathy. No reveal. Makes the audience invested in whether she posts it. Comments will beg her to.

**CTA:** None. Pure anticipation build.

---

**Format:** Bold-text graphic on black background. Big typography.

**Why it works:** Commits to a date (urgency). Hints at scope (12 posts = a real body of work, not a one-off). Promises vulnerability without describing it.

**CTA:** "Turn notifications on."
