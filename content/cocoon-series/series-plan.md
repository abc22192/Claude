## Tone variety requirement (per her ask)

> *"I think throwing in some emotional parts but also vulnerable things like that but also making it with some comedy with some strong confident types. It's really what I need. I need to be a good variety."*

Mix across the 12 posts:
- **Emotional:** The Sleepover, "Go wash" — Three Years of a Narcissist
- **Vulnerable:** Lipedema reveal if she opts in, Booty Juice: An Origin Story
- **Comedy:** The Farting Game Suspension, The Impossible Sandals, The 17-Wrap Poop Package
- **Strong-confident:** The First Fan Who Paid For It, A Day In the Life of a Sock-Less Woman, The Day My Feet Became a Business

Not all one register. Each week should hit at least 2 registers across the two posts.
