# Daily Clarity

Read `context/operating-manual.md` and `context/current-priorities.md`. Then:

1. Surface the 1-3 actions today that actually move the user's goals. Not a long list.
2. Name the single highest-leverage one. If it is the scary or avoided kind (per their failure modes), say so directly.
3. Keep it to action, not a plan.

Do not generate busywork. If the user's instinct is to organize, build, or research instead of doing the real thing, flag it against their stated failure modes. The point of clarity is movement on what matters, not a tidy list.
