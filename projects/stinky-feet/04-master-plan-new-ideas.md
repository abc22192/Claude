### Idea 1: The Wear Diary Draft

**What it is.** Your followers vote on what you wear next. "Quick question… white socks or black socks for the next 5 days?" The winning pair becomes a public diary: one Stinky Feet Report per day, day count on every post. On the last day it sells with a proof video, and the people who voted get first dibs for 24 hours before it goes up on ATW or mymember.site.

**How it's different from what you have.** Your Shoe Sponsorship idea (a monthly pair made to one fan's stink level, with first purchase rights) is private and one-to-one. The Diary is public and voted on by everyone. That makes the diary your content calendar, and every vote is a reply.

**Why it works:**
- **People value what they helped make.** In the IKEA effect studies, people were willing to pay more for things they assembled themselves than for the same things already built (Norton, Mochon & Ariely 2012, *Journal of Consumer Psychology* 22(3):453-460). Voters helped make this pair.
- **More contact means more value.** Changing how much physical contact a person had with an object changed how much buyers wanted it (Newman, Diesendruck & Bloom 2011, *Journal of Consumer Research* 38(2):215-228). Real celebrity auction prices showed the same pattern (Newman & Bloom 2014, *PNAS*). Each diary day adds value, which is the same logic as your Day Dial store ("they're not buying socks, they're buying days").
- **Votes are replies.** X's 2023 ranking code weighted a reply at 13.5 and a like at 0.5.
- **Your own data.** Your best X post was a choice question, and it got 43 replies.

**How to run it:**
1. Use socks or memory-foam insoles. You already do custom socks at +$10 a day with daily photos, and insoles hold scent well. Save shoes for a finale, since only about 2 pairs a year are worth sending (your Worn Items index).
2. Post the vote.
3. Post 1 photo and 1 line per day (day count plus one detail).
4. Last day: seal it in 2 bags, film the proof video, and open first dibs to voters for 24 hours.
5. Price it with your per-day logic.

**Cost:** $0 beyond the socks. **Time:** about 10 minutes a day during a run.
**Risk:** X limits reach on adult media and your account gets banned. Keep diary posts suggestive and mirror every entry to Tellegramz and email.
**How you'll know:** replies on the vote post, hours to sell, and the final price compared with the same item sold cold.

### Idea 2: Sniff Cards

**What it is.** A $9 to $12 entry item: a cotton square worn against your dry soles and toe jam, sealed the moment it comes off, mailed flat with a QR code to a private proof clip. It's the first physical thing a shy buyer can say yes to.

**Why it works:**
- **Small yes, then bigger yes.** People who agree to a small request are much more likely to agree to a bigger one later (Freedman & Fraser 1966, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*).
- **You have a price gap.** Your cheapest physical feet item is foot dust at $27 to $45 (your two price pages disagree), and worn items cost more. On Beacons, the items that sell cost $5 to $13. Nothing physical sits under $27.
- **Cotton is your proven material.** Your inhaler notes say nothing held scent the way cotton does.
- **The stink chemistry is known.** Foot odor is isovaleric acid, made when skin bacteria break down leucine in sweat. Citral, citronellal, and geraniol (citrus, lemongrass, and rose-geranium scents) block it (Ara et al. 2006, *Canadian Journal of Microbiology*). So nothing citrus-scented goes near the packing.
- **Cheap to mail.** A flat 1 oz letter mails with one Forever stamp, $0.82 since July 12, 2026. A lumpy or rigid pouch costs more, so keep it flat.

**How to run it:**
1. Make 10 at once.
2. Seal each one immediately (your "sealed before it sees daylight" rule).
3. Make the QR codes in Beacons (it has a QR code generator) and point them at a private proof clip.
4. Sell through mymember.site (it already sells your used items and runs CCBill) and put a button on the Beacons Feet tab that links there. US-only to start, matching your ATW shipping terms.
5. Seven days after each sale, email the buyer one bigger item: socks, insoles, or foot dust.

**Cost:** cotton squares, pouches, and a stamp, low single digits per card. **Time:** 1 hour per batch of 10.
**Risk:** it could pull buyers down from bigger items. The 7-day follow-up is there to pull them back up.
**How you'll know:** cards sold, and how many card buyers buy something bigger within 30 days.

The rule is simple: credit you and link your profile. Artists make giantess art of you, and every piece carries your name to their followers. Later you sell premium stock packs.

- **It's allowed.** DeviantArt permits mature, non-pornographic fetish themes when they're labeled mature, and bans pornography showing real people (DeviantArt Help Center). SFW sole photos are inside the line.
- **There's a way to get paid later.** DeviantArt Core members can sell Premium Downloads and Premium Galleries (DeviantArt Help Center).
- **The trend is live.** The fastest-growing channel in your YouTube competitor scan is giantess and shrinking POV (KillerGirlSoles, views up 120% in 30 days).
- **Your dormant DeviantArt account gets exactly one job.**

**How to run it:**
1. Shoot 10 low-angle sole photos with the camera on the floor and toy cars or figures for scale.
2. Post 5 free, marked mature, in giantess groups. Put the license in the description: credit required, no nudity, no sexual edits.
3. Share the best artist pieces on X (with credit) as giantess Stinky Feet Reports.
4. After 30 days, if artists are using your stock, open a Premium Gallery with 20 more. That needs a paid Core plan, so pay only if the free drop worked.
5. Point your DeviantArt profile at your C4S giantess clips, after checking DeviantArt's link rules for mature profiles.

**Cost:** $0 to start. **Time:** 1 shoot, then 20 minutes a week.
**Risk:** someone makes explicit art of you. Your license forbids it, and DeviantArt bans real-person pornography, so you can report it.
**How you'll know:** how many pieces use your stock, DeviantArt profile visits, and your C4S giantess sales month over month.

### Bonus idea 4: Restink Refills

**What it is.** Your Revamp guide teaches buyers to bring the stink back into shoes they already own. Refills are the second step: a worn cotton insole liner they drop into their stored shoes to re-scent them, with a monthly option.

**Why it works:**
- The Revamp guide is your most-viewed product (132 views, 82 from X), so its buyers are proven worn-item people.
- Stink needs sweat and bacteria (Ara et al. 2006), and a fresh worn liner brings both.
- Replenishment subscriptions made up 32% of e-commerce subscriptions in McKinsey's study, and people pick them because they need the product and like a discount.
- Your notes have a "stinky shoe subscription" and "stinky sock renewal," which renew your items. Refills restink theirs.

**How to run it:** offer it in the Revamp guide's post-purchase message and price it between Sniff Cards and socks.
