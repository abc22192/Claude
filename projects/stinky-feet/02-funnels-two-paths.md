## 5. OnlyFans mechanics (for either path)

- **Price floor:** OnlyFans' minimum subscription price is $4.99. Your 40% promo puts the first month near $2.99, which is the "$3" you said. Check that the Promotions screen accepts it before you announce anything.
- **Promo rules of thumb from OnlyFans pricing guides:** stay at or under 50% off, run it 48 to 72 hours, and don't do it more than 4 to 6 times a year. Your 40% fits.
- **Trial links:** you set the offer limit (your 5 people), the expiration, and the trial length. When the 5 spots are gone, the link stops working.
- **Your rule checks out:** pay-per-view posts on the wall only work on free pages. A paid VIP page sells PPV through DMs.
- **Rolling 30 (fixes "the VIP gives away too much"):** this is your own play from the Sept 12 OnlyFans OS (Lesson 1.5): archive, never delete, keep about 20 to 30 live posts per page, and release in staggered drops so a leaker only ever gets a slice. Your plan does the fart page first and the feet page second. Keep about 30 days live on the feet VIP and sell older sets back as PPV bundles in DMs.
- **One thing to decide:** your OnlyFans OS says the open page works best as previews and leads only, with no PPV on its wall. Today you described locked PPV on the open page's timeline. Both can work, so pick one on purpose.
- **Leak defense you already have:** OnlyFans watermarks content with your username and runs a DMCA team. Paid takedown services exist, but only pay for one if leaks are costing you more than the fee.
