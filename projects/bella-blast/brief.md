## What This Is

The Bella Blast is Bella's signature content offering — a hyper-real, niche-fetish day experience built around:

- Bella eating a custom meal (chosen together with the buyer)
- Documenting everything that happens to her body after — gas, bloating, belly, smell descriptions, all of it
- Sending real-time video clips and messages throughout the entire day
- Being completely unfiltered: belly out, imperfections visible, nothing staged
- The buyer experiences the day WITH her, not just receives content

The pitch: "This is like dating me. You're living this day with me from wherever you are."

- **Open question:** Keep this name? Notion flagged it's close to "Bella Effect" — may cause brand confusion

### Blast Day — The Full Experience
- **Price: $100 + meal (buyer covers Uber Eats)**
- Bella and buyer pick the meal together
- Bella screenshots the Uber Eats order so buyer sees exact cost before paying
- Bella eats on video, records digestion, sends all-day real-time clips
- Content: gas, farts, belly bloating, smell descriptions, imperfections — nothing held back
- "You'll see my belly hanging out. I don't care. You're gonna see me in all my imperfections."
- One person per day — hard limit

### Blast Experience — The Girlfriend Experience
- **Price: $150 + meal / $125 without food**
- Full GFE: all-day messaging from wake-up to bedtime
- Schedule syncing — they live the same day she does
- High volume: "You'll beg me to stop. I'll send you so much."
- Food component is OPTIONAL — some buyers just want the GFE
- She gets their schedule and preferences before the day starts

### Extended Options (message for pricing — not on main menu)
- 3-day package
- 1-week package
- No monthly options
