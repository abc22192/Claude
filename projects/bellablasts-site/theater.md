# bellablasts.com — The Theater

The site is a real theater on your phone. No scrolling anywhere. No kink walls. You tap, a view opens, you tap to leave. Three layers: the Marquee, the Lobby, the Rooms.

---

## The whole site at a glance

- **Marquee** → one entrance screen, no decisions, just atmosphere
- **Lobby** → one screen with 5 posters on red velvet walls, each a different "room"
- **Rooms** → one full-screen view per poster, each self-contained, "X" to return

Every screen fits one phone, 480px max width. Zero scrolling anywhere.

## Rules the whole site follows

- Zero scrolling. Every view = one phone screen.
- No kink gates. Every room mixes niches.
- Only ONE form on the site: the Bella Effect application. It already filters.
- Mobile-first, 480px max. Desktop centers in frame with velvet border.
- Palette: near-black `#0a0a0a`, red velvet `#4a0e13`, rose gold `#C8A08C`, warm candlelight glow
- Fonts: Cinzel (signs, headings) + Tenor Sans (body)
- Every button routes to a destination that handles its own checkout. You screen almost no one.
