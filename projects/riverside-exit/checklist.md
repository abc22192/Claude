## The one rule

Do not click Cancel until the files are downloaded and you have opened a few of them to check they play.

Sources disagree on what happens at cancel. Riverside's own blog says cancelling is immediate. Other guides say you keep paid features until the billing period ends. Free accounts may lose separate-track downloads, editing, and full export quality. Treat the files as the only thing that counts.

## Order of operations

1. **Plan & Billing first.** Settings, then Plan & Billing. Write down the plan name, the price, and the renewal date. Screenshot it. Do not click Cancel.
2. **Download raw tracks.** Per recording: Recordings, then Tracks, then Download. Do Godiva and Asian Vibes first. Pro and Business plans can download a zip of all tracks for a session. Lower plans may not, so do this while you are still paid up.
3. **Export finished edits at the best quality your plan gives.** Export quality is tied to the plan. Public sources say the free tier caps at 720p with a watermark, and I could not confirm 480p anywhere. Either way, export before the plan changes.
4. **Put everything in ONE Google Drive folder** named "Riverside Exit Oct 2026". Then a second copy somewhere else, like an external drive. One copy is not a backup.
5. **Check three random files.** Open them, confirm the length is right and the audio is clear.
6. **Then cancel.** If you need Riverside again for a big batch, sign up for one month at that time.

## Captivate (your actual podcast host)

- Leave it alone. It is where your RSS feed and episode files live, and the PodGAS site embeds that feed.
- Do not cancel Captivate first. Cancelling a podcast host usually takes the feed and the audio files down with it. Moving hosts goes the other way: new host first, import or redirect the feed, wait until Apple and Spotify have followed, THEN cancel the old one.
- Riverside hosting would cost more than Captivate, not less. Captivate is about $17 to $19 a month on the plans I could find.

## What Claude can and cannot do here

Can:
- List and sort what is in the account (done above)
- Start the export renders for the finished edits once you say go
- Save transcripts (plain text) for every real recording into Notion or this repo
- Keep this checklist and tell you what is left

Cannot:
- Download big media files for you. This sandbox blocks Riverside's download address (private-assets.riverside.com) unless you add it under Allowed domains in the environment's network settings. Even then, the export tool only returns a storage key and not a link, and the Google Drive connector would have to pass the whole file through chat, which does not work for 90 minutes of audio or video. Downloads have to go from Riverside, to your computer or phone, to Drive.
- Listen to audio or check quality. That is on you.
