## 7. HYPE STRATEGY — When and How to Use It

**Status: She's eligible. She just hasn't activated the system.**

Eligibility confirmed:
- PodGasFarts has 2,370 subscribers — within the 500–500,000 band
- Channel appears to have crossed YPP threshold around Sept 2025
- Long-form videos (standard uploads) are eligible — Shorts are NOT

**The key fact:** At her subscriber count, each Hype is worth a dramatically larger point multiplier than a Hype on a big channel. YouTube built this intentionally to surface emerging creators. One fan Hype on her channel moves the needle more than the same action on a channel with 500K subscribers.

### The 7-day window — the most important operational fact:
Hype ONLY works on videos published in the last 7 days. The button disappears after day 7. This means:
- Every new upload starts a 7-day Hype clock
- Best time to ask for Hypes: within the first 24–48 hours of posting
- Build the Hype ask INTO every new video as a standard element

### How to implement:

**In every new video:**
Use the Hype script (already written and production-ready):
> "Real quick before you go — YouTube has this feature called Hype. You get three of them every week, completely free. No upgrade, no payment, nothing. They reset every Monday. What makes it worth asking about: smaller channels get a bigger multiplier per hype than big ones. One hype on my channel moves the needle more than the same tap on a channel with a million subscribers. Those points go toward a leaderboard that new people browse — people who've never heard of me. One catch: it only works within the first seven days of a video going up. So if you're watching this while it's still new — use one of your hypes right now, babe. Before you close the tab. That's it. Thank you for real."

**Pinned comment (paste within 24 hours of posting):**
> "If you're watching this in the first week — hit Hype. Button's right below the like button, totally free. YouTube gives everyone 3 hypes per week and they reset Monday. For a channel my size the multiplier is real, it helps new people find me, and it takes you two seconds."

**Description blurb (add to every new video description):**
> "If this video is less than 7 days old, tap HYPE — it's free, you get 3 per week, and it puts me on a leaderboard where new people find this content."

**Cross-post to Telegram:** Her Telegram members are already fans — they're the most likely to act on the ask. Announce every new upload there within an hour of it going live, with the Hype ask included.

**Posting day strategy:** Post Monday or Tuesday. That gives the 7-day window maximum overlap with the weekend when fans have the most time to watch and engage.
