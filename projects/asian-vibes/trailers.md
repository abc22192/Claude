## What I did about the talking over each other and the sound

- The two voices are on separate tracks in this recording, which is why this is fixable.
- I only picked lines where the other person is not talking inside the window. I checked this against the timing data for all 7 trailers. Nobody overlaps inside any piece.
- Closest call is the very end of trailer 5. Asian Vibes finishes "power exchange" and Bella's "Yeah" starts 28 milliseconds later. Listen to that last word.
- I skipped one spot on purpose in trailer 6 where Asian Vibes says "Breaking" right over Bella.
- The smart mutes from the earlier audio rescue carry over.
- I turned Magic Audio on for both voice tracks in all 7 trailers. Riverside reports it as ready.
