## CRITICAL: Fix Before Selling a Single Square

### 1. The Compliance Agent's gambling fix changes the ENTIRE event pitch
**The problem:** Compliance (Agent 5) recommends Option A: give every square buyer a wheel spin regardless of whether the fart lands in their window. This removes the gambling element. But Voice Master (Agent 1), Live Director (Agent 4), and Marketing (Agent 7) ALL wrote their posts and plans around the original structure where only the "winning" window holder gets a spin.

**What to fix:** If Bella takes the compliance recommendation (she should), every post, the live show script, and the marketing pitch need to be rewritten to reflect: "Buy a square, get the recording AND a wheel spin. The live event is entertainment. The prize is guaranteed with purchase."

This actually makes the pitch STRONGER for buyers (guaranteed prize vs. chance-based), but the current Voice Master posts sell uncertainty-as-the-prize, not the guaranteed wheel spin.

**Decision needed from Bella:** Original structure (keep the gambling risk) or Option A (everyone spins, no risk)?

### 2. The website doesn't exist yet and square sales can't open without it
**The problem:** Content Scheduler (Agent 3) has square sales opening Oct 25-26 (Phase 2). Website Architect (Agent 2) estimates 2-3 days to build plus DNS propagation. That's tight.

**What to fix:** Either:
- Start the website build TODAY (Oct 19) so it's ready by Oct 23-24
- OR: sell squares through DMs first (no website needed) and launch the website later as a visual status board

**My recommendation:** Start building today. But have a DM-only backup plan: she can sell squares through direct messages even if the website isn't ready. The website is nice-to-have for visual appeal, not required for the transaction.

### 3. No one addressed: what happens if the same fan buys squares on BOTH sides?
A VIP OF subscriber could buy a $10 square through OF, then find the open side and buy another $10 square through Cash App. Is that fine? Is it encouraged? Does it create confusion?

**Fix:** Add a line to the FAQ / event terms: "You can buy multiple squares across platforms. Each square is independent. Multiple windows = multiple chances."

## HIGH PRIORITY: Gaps Across Agents

### 4. Voice Master posts don't include the compliance-safe language
None of the 8 posts mention event terms, refund policy, or eligibility language. Compliance says to write basic event terms (Change #6). At minimum, every sales post should include or link to: "No refunds. All purchases include the full recording. Must be 18+."

**Fix:** Add one line to every sales-oriented post (Posts 1, 2, 3): "All sales final. Full recording guaranteed."

### 5. Live Director assumes original gambling structure throughout
The show rundown (Agent 4) is built entirely around "if a fart happens during YOUR window, you win." If the structure changes to "everyone gets a spin," the show flow needs adjusting:
- The window-by-window suspense is still entertainment (will it happen NOW?), but the spin happens for everyone at the end
- The "What If: no fart in any window" scenario changes: no consolation needed because everyone already gets a spin
- The wheel moment becomes a group event at the end, not individual wins throughout the show

### 6. Pricing Agent says "no cap on buyers per square" but nobody addressed what that actually means
If 10 people buy Window A and a fart happens during Window A, do all 10 win a wheel spin? That's 10 custom videos or free months. Under the compliance-safe version (everyone spins), this becomes: 10 people per window x 6 windows = potentially 60 wheel spins.

**Fix:** Either cap buyers per window (creates scarcity and urgency) or accept unlimited and plan for prize delivery at scale. Recommendation: cap at 3 per window. "Only 3 spots per window. 18 total spots across all six." This creates real urgency and keeps prize delivery manageable.

### 7. No one built the actual board / square tracking system
Website Architect designed it. But who builds it? The JSON file, PHP endpoint, admin page, DNS setup... that's actual development work. None of the agents assigned it or estimated build time beyond "2-3 days."

**Fix:** This is the first build task after Bella approves. It needs to be scheduled and started.

### 8. The "HALLOWEEN" keyword has no system behind it
Marketing (Agent 7) and Voice Master (Agent 1) both use "message me HALLOWEEN" as the funnel entry point. But there's no tracking system. Who messaged? What platform? Did they buy? When?

**Fix:** She needs a simple tracking method. Even a phone note:
- Name | Platform | Messaged date | Bought? | Window | Payment confirmed?

Without this, she'll lose track of interested fans and miss follow-ups.
