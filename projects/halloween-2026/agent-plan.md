## AGENT 2: Website Architect
**Job:** Plan the dual-payment website for Blast or Treat

**The problem Bella is solving:** OnlyFans only allows OnlyFans payment processing when you link from their platform. But she has fans coming from Telegram, Twitter, and everywhere else who want to pay with Cash App, YouPay, or Amazon gift cards.

**The solution -- two faces, one event:**

| Side | Who sees it | Payment | Where it's linked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OF-Clean Side** | OnlyFans audience | OnlyFans payment ONLY (PPV, tip, or OF native) | Posted directly on OnlyFans (free + VIP pages) |
| **Open Side** | Everyone else (Telegram, Twitter, IG, direct) | Cash App, YouPay, Amazon gift card | Linked from Telegram, Twitter, website, everywhere except OF |

**What this agent plans:**
- Page structure for both sides (same event board, different payment paths)
- How the shared square board works across both
- URL routing: bellablasts.com/halloween (open) vs. a clean OF-compliant version
- Mobile-first, black + rose gold #C8A08C, Cinzel/Tenor Sans
- Theater aesthetic (doors, rooms, atmosphere -- NOT a storefront)
- How fans from each platform find and pay for their squares
- Integration with manual verification workflow from the Notion plan

**What it does NOT do:**
- Build anything (plan only)
- Auto-verify payments (Bella checks manually)
- Combine payment methods on one page (keeps them separated for compliance)

## AGENT 5: Compliance Watchdog
**Job:** Keep everything legal and platform-safe

**The known risk (from Notion plan):** Bella is in Virginia. Paying for a chance to receive a reward tied to an uncertain live event raises gambling/sweepstakes questions under Virginia Code 18.2-325.

**What this agent does:**
- Reviews every piece of content, pricing, and promotion from the other agents
- Flags anything that could violate:
  - Virginia gambling/sweepstakes law
  - OnlyFans Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy
  - Telegram terms
  - Twitter/X content policies
  - Instagram community guidelines (for the safe BBW posts)
- Proposes compliant alternatives when something flags
- Checks that the two-payment-system website doesn't violate OF terms (no routing OF buyers to outside payment)
- Confirms the "everyone gets recording + chance at bonus prize" structure
- Keeps a running compliance checklist

**What it recommends (from the Notion plan, already flagged):**
- Confirm format with OnlyFans and Telegram before selling
- Consult Virginia counsel on the wheel/prize mechanic
- Never disguise payment routing or mislead platform reviewers
- Keep the no-purchase-necessary question open until legal review

**What it does NOT do:**
- Provide legal advice (it flags, Bella decides with real counsel)
- Kill the fun -- it finds the compliant path, not the "don't do it" path

## How They Work Together

```
Agent 1 (Voice) writes the posts
    |
Agent 2 (Website) builds the payment/board plan
    |
Agent 3 (Scheduler) organizes when everything goes out
    |
Agent 4 (Live Director) plans the actual event
    |
Agent 5 (Compliance) reviews ALL of the above
    |
Agent 6 (Pricing) locks in the money structure
    |
Agent 7 (Marketing) wraps everything in a campaign
    |
Agent 8 (Gap Finder) reviews ALL agents' output, catches what everyone missed
```

**Order of operations:**
1. Compliance (Agent 5) and Pricing (Agent 6) run FIRST -- no point writing posts if the structure changes
2. Website (Agent 2) and Voice (Agent 1) run in parallel once structure is locked
3. Scheduler (Agent 3) and Marketing (Agent 7) build on top of 1 and 2
4. Live Director (Agent 4) runs independently (it's about the event itself)
5. Gap Finder (Agent 8) runs LAST -- reviews everything
