# Blast or Treat — Content Schedule
_Agent 3: Content Scheduler | Built 2026-10-19_
_This is not a calendar. It's a game plan with nudges._

---

## How This Works

**Four phases. Eight platforms. Pre-written posts.**

You don't have to think about what to write. Voice Master already wrote the posts. Your job is approving them and hitting send at roughly the right time.

No exact timestamps. No "post at 9:47am." Just three time blocks: **morning** (before noon), **afternoon** (noon to 6), **evening** (after 6). Pick whatever feels right within the block.

**The one rule:** Never go more than 2 days dark on any active platform. If you haven't posted somewhere in 48 hours, grab the emergency filler for that platform (listed at the bottom) and send it.

## Cross-Promotion Map

How fans move between platforms:

```
Twitter (public) → DM → OF or Telegram signup
Free OF → VIP OF upgrade ($10 vs $15 pricing as incentive)
Free Telegram → Vault signup (VIFL or VISL)
PodGAS audience → Twitter → DM → OF or Telegram
IG → doesn't link directly (safe content only), but builds curiosity
Curvage / ScatBook → OF or Telegram (mention in existing posts)
```

**The "HALLOWEEN" keyword** ties everything together. Every platform drives to the same action: message her HALLOWEEN. She can then route them to the right payment method based on where they came from.

## The Nudge System

**Not alarms. Not schedules. Just three check-ins per phase.**

**Phase 1 check-in (around Oct 21):** "Have I posted the hype tease on at least 3 platforms? If not, do that today."

**Phase 2 check-in (around Oct 28):** "How many squares are sold? Have I done the full reveal on all main platforms? If not, today's the day."

**Phase 3 check-in (Oct 31 morning):** "Is the setup ready? Last-chance post going out? Squares confirmed? Good. Breathe. You've got this."

**Phase 4 check-in (Nov 2):** "Have recordings been delivered? Thank-you posts done? Winner prize set up? If yes, you crushed it."
