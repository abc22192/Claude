# Blast or Treat Content Scheduler
_Agent 3: Content Scheduler | Built 2026-10-19_
_Not a calendar. A system of blocks she moves when she's ready._

---

## How This Works

Everything below is organized by **phase**, not by day. Each phase has a bucket of things to post. She picks them up when she has energy, drops them when she doesn't. The only hard rule: **never go more than 2 days dark on any platform.**

Posts are pre-written (Voice Master, Agent 1). She approves once, then it's copy-paste-send whenever she's ready.

**Time blocks, not clock times:**
- Morning = before 1pm
- Afternoon = 1pm to 6pm
- Evening = after 6pm

She posts when she posts. These are just labels so she knows which window a post fits best in.

## THE 2-DAY RULE

This is the only hard rule in the whole system: **never go more than 2 days without posting something on any active platform.**

"Something" can be:
- A reshare of an existing pinned post
- A one-line square update ("Window C just sold")
- An IG story (phone camera, 5 seconds)
- A reply to a comment or DM that turns into a public post

It does not have to be new. It does not have to be long. It just has to exist.

**If she's crashing (2-day sleep collapse, bad day, no energy):**
The lowest-effort option is resharing a pinned post with zero new caption. That counts. It keeps the algorithm alive and the audience aware. Do that, then rest.
