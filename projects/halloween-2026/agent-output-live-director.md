## PRE-SHOW — 15 Minutes Before Go Time

### The "Going Live" Post (send on both platforms)
> Getting into costume. Blast or Treat goes live in 15 minutes. If you bought a square, your window is coming. If you didn't... there's still time. Last squares available in chat. See you soon.

Short, confident, no begging. She's the host of the party, not someone hoping people show up.

### Setup Checklist (do this in order)

- [ ] Phone/laptop #1: OnlyFans live ready, NOT started yet
- [ ] Phone/laptop #2: Telegram video ready, NOT started yet
- [ ] Local recording running on BOTH devices (screen record or OBS on laptop, built-in record on phone). This is your backup. Start recording BEFORE you go live. Do not rely on platform recordings alone.
- [ ] Camera angle set: waist-up or full body, whatever you're comfortable with. Stable surface or tripod. No hand-holding for an hour.
- [ ] Lighting: main light on your face (ring light or lamp), accent light behind you for atmosphere. If you have color-changing bulbs, orange or purple glow behind. Even a string of dollar-store Halloween lights draped behind you adds vibe.
- [ ] Costume ON and checked in camera
- [ ] Wheel ready and within arm's reach (see Wheel section below)
- [ ] Square board open on a separate screen/tab so you can glance at who owns which window
- [ ] Water bottle nearby. You're talking for an hour.
- [ ] Timer app open on your phone or a visible clock. You need to track 10-minute windows.
- [ ] Chat notifications turned OFF on everything except the two live platforms. No random dings.

### Energy Warm-Up (seriously, do this)
Put on music that makes you feel powerful for 10 minutes while you set up. Not relaxing music. The music that makes you walk like you own the building. Your energy when you hit "go live" sets the tone for the whole show. If you start low, the chat starts low. If you start like Halloween is YOUR holiday, they'll match it.

## THE OPENING — First 5 Minutes

### How You Start
Go live on both platforms within 30 seconds of each other. OnlyFans first (larger audience), Telegram immediately after.

First words on camera. Don't overthink this. Something like:

> "Happy Halloween. Welcome to Blast or Treat. If you're here, you already know what's about to happen. If you don't... you're about to find out."

Pause. Let people file in. Read the chat for a minute. Say hi to names you recognize. Let the room fill up.

### Explain the Game (keep it under 2 minutes)
Not everyone will remember the rules even if they bought a square. Quick version:

> "Here's how this works. We have six ten-minute windows. A through F. If you bought a square, you picked a window. At some point during this hour, a fart is going to happen. Nobody knows when. Not even me. If it happens during YOUR window, you win. I spin the wheel, and whatever it lands on is yours. Custom video, unreleased content, or a free month. Everyone who bought a square gets the recording of this whole thing whether you win or not. And if you didn't buy a square, you can still grab one right now, or just hang out and tip. Let's go."

### Build the Atmosphere
- Acknowledge the Halloween vibe: "I went all out tonight. Look at this setup."
- Show off the costume. Let them react. Read the compliments. Be playful about it.
- If you have Halloween decorations visible, gesture at them.
- Drop one line that sets the tone: "This is the one night where everything I do is on theme."

### Start the Clock
> "Window A starts NOW. Ten minutes on the clock. Let's see what happens."

## WINDOW-BY-WINDOW FLOW

### The Rhythm of Each Window

Every window follows the same loose structure. Don't script it word for word. Just know the beats:

**Opening of the window (first 1-2 minutes):**
- Announce it: "We are now in Window [letter]. [Alias name], this is YOUR window."
- If multiple buyers in the same window: "Window C belongs to [alias] and [alias]. Both of you are watching right now."
- Chat engagement: "How's everyone feeling? You think it's gonna happen this round?"

**Middle of the window (minutes 3-7):**
- This is where you LIVE. Talk, joke, react to chat, move around, be yourself.
- Conversation topics to rotate through:
  - Halloween stories: "What's the worst Halloween costume you've ever seen?"
  - Ask chat questions: "If you could pick any prize off the wheel, which one would you want?"
  - Tease the tension: shift in your seat, make a face, pause mid-sentence... then laugh. "Not yet."
  - Read and respond to tips in real time. Every tip gets a thank-you by name.
  - Talk about your costume, how you picked it, what almost happened instead
  - Light domme energy: "You're all just sitting there waiting for me to do something I can't even control. And you paid for it. I love this."

**End of the window (last 2-3 minutes):**
- Build it up: "Two minutes left in Window [letter]. [Alias], you feeling lucky?"
- Let the chat get loud. Read their reactions.
- If nothing happens: "Window [letter] is CLOSED. [Alias], not your round. But there are still [X] windows left."
- Move immediately to the next: "Window [next letter] starts NOW."

### If a Fart Happens During a Window

This is THE moment. Don't rush it. Let it land.

1. **React naturally.** You don't need to announce it like a game show host. Your face will do the work. A grin, a laugh, an "oh no."
2. **Confirm it:** "That just happened. Window [letter]. [Alias], you just WON."
3. **Let the chat lose their minds for 30 seconds.** Read the reactions. Enjoy it.
4. **Go to the wheel:** "Let's see what [alias] is taking home tonight."
5. **Spin the wheel** (see Wheel section below).
6. **Announce the prize.** Congratulate them. Be genuinely excited for them.
7. **Continue the show.** This doesn't end the live. Other windows still play out. The tension just shifts: "That was ONE. Who knows if there's another one coming?"

### If a Window Passes Without a Fart

This is GOOD. This is what makes the game work. The tension builds with every empty window.

- Keep it light: "Nothing. Window [letter], you're safe... or unlucky, depending on how you look at it."
- Build the pressure: "That's [X] windows down and nothing yet. Which means it's building. Whoever has a later window might want to pay attention."
- Don't apologize. Don't act disappointed. The waiting IS the entertainment.

### Keeping Non-Buyers Engaged

People watching without squares still matter. They tip, they chat, they tell friends.

- Acknowledge them: "Even if you don't have a square, you're part of this. The energy in here matters."
- Tip callouts: "Someone just tipped [amount]! Thank you, [name]. You might not have a square but you've got taste."
- Last-chance square pushes (casually, not desperately): "If anyone wants in, there are still [X] squares open. DM me the window letter."
- Chat interaction: ask questions, respond to comments, make people feel like they're at the party even without a ticket to the VIP table.

## THE WHEEL

### Recommendation: Physical Wheel

A real, physical wheel she spins on camera. Here's why:
- It's more dramatic. You can see it spinning. The click-click-click sound builds tension.
- It's trustworthy. No one can claim a digital wheel was rigged.
- It's content. The visual of Bella spinning a wheel in a Halloween costume is a clip unto itself.

**How to make one cheaply:**
- Buy a small tabletop prize wheel (Amazon, $15-30, search "dry erase prize wheel" or "spinning wheel game"). Many come blank so you can write your own options.
- Write three sections: CUSTOM VIDEO | UNRELEASED CONTENT | FREE MONTH
- Keep it close to camera. When it's time, hold it up or spin it on a table where the camera can see.

**Backup if no physical wheel:**
- Use a free wheel spinner site (wheelofnames.com or similar) and share screen / hold phone to camera
- Or: write the three prizes on cards, shuffle them face down, and pick one. Less dramatic but functional.

### The Spin Moment

1. Hold the wheel up. "This is what we're playing for."
2. Name the three prizes out loud so everyone knows the stakes.
3. "Ready, [alias]?" Pause. Let them respond in chat.
4. SPIN. Let it ride. Don't talk over the spin. Let the sound (or the visual) do the work.
5. When it lands: "It's... [prize]!" React. Be excited. Be jealous. Be playful. "You lucky [term of endearment]. A whole custom, just for you."
6. Tell them you'll DM after the show to set it up. Move on to the next window.

## COSTUME / SETUP IDEAS

### Costume Concepts (BBW + fart content + Halloween)

1. **Wicked Witch / Dark Sorceress**
   The classic. Long black dress or robe, dramatic makeup, maybe a hat. The joke writes itself: "I'm brewing something." Elegant, on-brand (black), and she can play up the "dark magic" angle all night. A cauldron prop would be perfect for the wheel moment.

2. **Vampire / Vampiress**
   Black and red. Cape. Fangs optional. Works beautifully with the rose gold accents. "I don't bite... but I might do something worse." Dominant energy built in.

3. **Devil**
   Red horns, red lips, black outfit. Simple and devastating. The Halloween fart live hosted by the devil is a clip title that writes itself. Pitchfork prop doubles as a pointer for the square board.

4. **Black Cat**
   Ears, tail, all black. Playful and a little mischievous. "Cats don't apologize for anything." Easy to put together, comfortable for an hour on camera, and the tail is a visual gag waiting to happen.

5. **Fortune Teller / Mystic**
   Headscarf, jewelry, dramatic eye makeup, flowing fabric. Leans into the "nobody knows when it'll happen" uncertainty of the game. "The cards say... Window D." Fits the vibe of prediction and mystery perfectly. A crystal ball prop next to the wheel would be incredible.

### Lighting / Backdrop

- **Main light:** Ring light or bright lamp aimed at her face. Non-negotiable for looking good on camera.
- **Background:** Keep it simple. A dark wall or curtain works. Add:
  - Orange or purple string lights (dollar store)
  - A few fake candles (LED, not real, she's moving around)
  - Jack-o-lantern or skull prop visible behind her
- **Color wash:** If she has a smart bulb or colored lamp, purple or deep orange behind her. This one detail makes the whole stream look intentional.
- **Keep it dark-ish.** The Halloween vibe is NOT bright and cheerful. Moody. Lit enough to see her clearly but atmospheric enough to feel like a Halloween event.

### Props That Work

- The wheel (see above)
- A timer visible on screen (phone propped up, or a countdown overlay if she's on laptop)
- A simple board or card showing the window schedule (A-F with times) so viewers can follow along
- A small Halloween prop near her (skull, pumpkin, candles) that becomes part of the set dressing
- A bell, horn, or noisemaker for the "WINNER" moment (optional, but adds fun)

## TECHNICAL CHECKLIST

### Dual Streaming

She's going live on OnlyFans AND Telegram at the same time. This means two separate streams.

**Option A: Two Devices (recommended for solo)**
- Device 1 (phone or laptop): OnlyFans live
- Device 2 (phone or laptop): Telegram live/video
- Both pointed at the same angle. Place them side by side on a shelf or table behind the camera.
- Prop them so both capture roughly the same frame.
- She talks to "the room," not to one device. Both audiences hear the same show.

**Option B: One Camera, Split Output**
- Requires OBS or similar streaming software on a laptop
- More complex, and if it fails, BOTH streams go down
- Not recommended for first time doing this

### Local Recording

- **Critical:** Start a screen recording or local camera recording BEFORE going live on either platform.
- On iPhone: use the built-in screen recorder (swipe to Control Center) on the device running the live
- On laptop: OBS, QuickTime (Mac), or built-in camera app
- On Android: built-in screen recorder
- This is the master copy. Platform recordings sometimes fail, get corrupted, or have lower quality. The local recording is her safety net.

### Internet Requirements

- Upload speed: minimum 5 Mbps for one stream, ideally 10+ for two simultaneous
- Run a speed test before going live (speedtest.net)
- Use WiFi, not cellular (unless cellular is faster and more stable)
- Close ALL other apps, tabs, and background downloads on both devices
- If possible, be the only person using the network during the live
- Keep one device plugged in. Running two live streams drains batteries fast.

### If Tech Fails Mid-Stream

**One stream drops, the other stays up:**
- Keep going on the surviving stream. Don't panic.
- Say: "Technical difficulty on [platform]. Hang tight, I'm getting it back."
- Restart the dropped stream. Rejoin the same live if the platform allows, or start a new one and post "Back!" in chat.
- The local recording keeps rolling regardless.

**Both streams drop:**
- Restart the stronger connection first (whichever device has better signal).
- Post in both chats: "Internet hiccup. I'm coming right back. Don't leave."
- Pick up where you left off. Reference the timer: "We were in Window [X], [minutes] left."
- If it's truly dead and won't come back: text/DM the square holders that you'll reschedule or deliver the recording of what happened. Don't ghost them.

**Audio/video quality is bad:**
- Switch to audio-only if video keeps buffering. "You can't see me but you can hear me, and honestly, hearing is the important part tonight."
- Move closer to the router if possible.

## "WHAT IF" SCENARIOS

### No fart happens during any window
This probably won't happen across a full hour, but just in case:
- Don't panic. Don't force it.
- After Window F closes: "Well... that was the most suspenseful hour of all our lives. Nobody won the wheel tonight."
- Give every square buyer a consolation: "Everyone who bought a square is getting a bonus. I'll DM you."
- The bonus could be an exclusive clip, a discount on next event, or a short custom message. Something that makes the no-win feel like a special outcome, not a rip-off.
- She still delivers the recording to everyone.

### Multiple farts in one window
- Each one is a win for whoever holds that window. "That's TWO in Window [letter]! [Alias], you're getting the wheel TWICE."
- Spin the wheel each time. Two different prizes (or the same, if it lands there).
- This is the best possible outcome for drama. Play it up.

### Multiple farts across different windows
- Perfect. Each window's holder gets their own spin.
- This spreads the excitement across the show and keeps everyone engaged.

### Low attendance
- Don't acknowledge it as "low." A small room is an intimate room.
- "This is a VIP night. Small crowd, big energy."
- Engage more personally with whoever is there. Use their names more. Make it feel exclusive because it IS.

### Someone tries to game the system
- Someone claims they paid when they didn't: "I check every payment manually. If yours isn't confirmed, your square isn't active. DM me the receipt."
- Someone tries to buy all the squares: Let them, if they're actually paying. That's just more revenue. Multiple wins means multiple spins.
- Someone demands a refund during the live: "I'll handle any questions after the show. Right now, we're playing."

### Late square purchase during the live
- "Yes! You can still grab a window. DM me which one you want and pay now. I'll confirm it in chat."
- Only sell windows that haven't started yet. Once a window is in progress, it's closed for new buyers.
- Keep it fast. Don't stop the show for a transaction. "I'll confirm in DM, keep watching."
