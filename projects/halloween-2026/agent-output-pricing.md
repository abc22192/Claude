**Total prize exposure per live (assuming 1-3 winners):**
- Best case (1 winner, unreleased video): $0 cost
- Worst case (3 winners, all customs): ~2 hours of work post-event
- Most likely: 1-2 winners, mix of prizes = ~30 min work + $0-$15 lost sub revenue

**This is low risk.** The wheel adds excitement for almost no real cost.

## 6. Tips During the Live

**Don't beg. Create moments.**

| Moment | Tip prompt (natural, not scripted) |
|---|---|
| Opening | "If you're excited for tonight, show me" (general vibe) |
| Between windows | "Who thinks the next one is THE one?" (builds anticipation) |
| After a fart lands | "That winner is getting [prize]... tip if you're jealous" (playful) |
| Empty window passes | "That window was quiet... should I make up for it?" (tease) |
| Closing | "Thanks for being here tonight" (genuine, no hard sell) |

**BELLA DECIDES: Set a tip goal?** Example: "If tips hit $100 tonight, I'll add a bonus 7th window." This creates collective energy. But only if she actually wants to do a 7th window.

## 7. Post-Event Replay

**BELLA DECIDES: Sell the replay separately?**

| Option | Price | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Include with purchase** (current plan) | $0 extra | Simple, everyone happy, no complaints | Leaves money on the table from people who missed the sale |
| **Sell replay after** | $10-$15 | Extra revenue, FOMO for next event | Admin work, could feel like double-dipping |
| **Replay as upsell only to non-buyers** | $20 | Premium price for those who missed out, rewards early buyers | Need to track who bought vs. didn't |

**My recommendation: Include with purchase (current plan), then offer the replay to non-buyers at $15 for 48 hours after the event.** Early buyers feel rewarded. Latecomers still get a path in. After 48 hours, it goes in the vault.

## 8. Is This Worth Her Time?

| Metric | Blast or Treat | Her other services |
|---|---|---|
| **Time investment** | ~1 hr live + ~2 hrs prep/promo + ~1 hr post-delivery = ~4 hrs total | -- |
| **LIKELY revenue** | ~$215 net | -- |
| **Hourly rate (likely)** | **~$54/hr** | -- |
| Fart call rate | -- | $30 for 5-7 min = **$257-$360/hr** |
| Bella Effect | -- | $25 for ~30 min = **$50/hr** |
| Blast Day | -- | $100 + meal for ~4 hrs = **$25/hr** |

**The honest read:** On pure hourly rate, the live event at LIKELY numbers pays about the same as a Bella Effect session and better than a Blast Day. It pays far less per hour than fart calls.

**But that misses the point.** The live event is not a service. It is:
- A **marketing event** that builds hype for everything else
- An **audience builder** that gives fans a shared experience and makes them more loyal
- A **content piece** (the recording becomes replay revenue and vault content)
- A **tip generator** on top of square sales
- A **proof of concept** for future live events at higher prices

At GOOD or BEST numbers ($400-$620 net), the hourly rate climbs to $100-$155/hr and the event clearly pays for itself. The question is whether she can hit those numbers with 12 days of promotion.

**Break-even for "worth my time":** If she values her time at $50/hr and the event takes 4 hours total, she needs $200 net. That is 14 squares at $10 + tips, or about the LIKELY scenario. Achievable.
