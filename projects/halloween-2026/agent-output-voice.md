## Voice Profile Summary

**4 modes used across these posts:**
- **Content Bella** — OF captions, Telegram vault posts, Twitter. Immediate, visceral, sensory. "You" dominant. Explicit where platform allows.
- **Sales Bella** — CTA-heavy conversion moments. Hook first, curiosity gap, value pivot.
- **Professional Bella** — PodGAS community. Warm authority, analogies, grounded vulnerability.
- **Mainstream Bella** — Instagram, PodGAS public promo. Smartest person at the dinner table. No fetish language.

**Rules baked into every post:**
- No em dashes. Ellipses for pauses. Complete sentences with varied structures.
- "You" as the subject of most sentences. One claim per line.
- Warmth leads, edge follows. Never the reverse.
- Zero banned words (see voice skill Section 7).
- CTA appears early AND mid-way in sales-oriented posts.
- Fart content: visual and body-focused. Never audio framing.
- Each post passed the self-audit checklist before inclusion.
