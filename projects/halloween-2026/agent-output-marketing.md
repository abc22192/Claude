## Cross-Pollination Strategy

How fans move between platforms during this campaign:

### Free OF → VIP OF
**The lever:** VIP gets squares at $10 vs. $15. That's a $5 savings per square, plus ongoing access to all VIP content.
**How to use it:** Every Free OF Blast or Treat post mentions VIP pricing. "VIP subscribers get a better price. You know where to find me."

### Twitter → OF or Telegram
**The lever:** Twitter can't show the full picture. DM HALLOWEEN creates a private conversation where she can send them directly to the right platform.
**How to use it:** Pin the reveal tweet. Reply to engagement with "DM me HALLOWEEN" instead of posting links publicly (Twitter suppresses posts with external links).

### Free Telegram → Vault
**The lever:** The vault gets the live stream. Free Telegram gets a teaser at best.
**How to use it:** Post vault links (VIFL, VISL) on every Free Telegram Halloween post. The live event is the best vault upgrade pitch she's ever had.

### PodGAS → Content Platforms
**The lever:** Podcast listeners trust her voice. The live event is the thing she talks about wanting to do.
**How to use it:** One organic cross-promo in PodGAS Telegram. One public tweet. Not a hard sell. Just awareness.

### Curvage / ScatBook / Clips4Sale → OF or Telegram
**The lever:** These are niche communities where she already has presence (182 Curvage posts, active ScatBook profile).
**How to use it:** If she's posting on these platforms during Oct 19-31 anyway, mention the Halloween event in any post or profile update. "Something special happening Oct 31. Find me on OnlyFans or Telegram." Don't make it the main content. Just a mention.

## The "HALLOWEEN" Keyword Mechanic

**How it works across platforms:**

1. Fan sees a post on any platform
2. Post says "message me HALLOWEEN"
3. Fan messages her the keyword
4. She knows they're interested
5. She routes them to payment based on where they already follow her:
   - Already on VIP OF? "Tip $10 with your window letter."
   - Already in Telegram vault? "Send $10 via Cash App/YouPay/Amazon."
   - On Free OF? "Tip $15 with your window letter. Or upgrade to VIP for $10."
   - On Twitter or no paid platform? "Here's my OF link or Telegram vault link. Sign up and you can grab a square."

**Why this works:** One keyword. Every platform. She doesn't have to write different CTAs for different platforms. The routing happens in the DM, not the post.

**Tracking:** Keep a note of everyone who messages HALLOWEEN. Follow up with anyone who expressed interest but hasn't bought by Oct 29.

**What it is:** A $30 phone/video call experience Bella offers.
