## WHEN THINGS GO WRONG

**When Claude makes a mistake:**
> That's wrong. Here's the correction: [X]. Save this as a permanent rule.

**When something was built wrong:**
> What we just built doesn't match what I asked for. Here's what I actually want: [X]. Before you rebuild, confirm you understand.

**When Claude is agreeing too much:**
> Stop agreeing with me. Give me your honest read on whether this is a good idea.

**When Claude misses the point:**
> You're missing the point. The actual problem is [X]. Start from there.

## SHORTCUTS THAT SAVE TIME

**Instead of explaining the whole situation:**

**Instead of re-explaining your voice:**
> Use my voice profile. Check bella-voice-profile.md.

**Instead of asking Claude to remember:**

**Instead of making Claude guess:**
