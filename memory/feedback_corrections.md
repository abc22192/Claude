## 2026-09-30: Never read brain dumps back for confirmation
- **Correction:** "Please don't confirm brain dumps. I don't like that. I'm not going to look at it anyways, too much shit."
- **Why:** The numbered readback costs her a round trip and a wall of text she won't read. It's friction, not help.

Put the parsed item list and any genuinely unresolved guesses inside the deliverable (a short "what I heard / assumptions" section), never as a pre-work confirmation gate. This overrides the synced `brain-dump` skill's Step 2 and Step 3 until she edits that skill.
